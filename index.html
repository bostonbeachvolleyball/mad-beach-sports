Know before you play

Rules

The goal is competitive volleyball without taking ourselves too seriously. Event-specific rules will always be shared before play starts.

Good volleyball. Good people.

Good sportsmanship is expected from players, spectators, and organizers. Calls happen fast. Be respectful, communicate, and keep the day moving.

Sportsmanship

Treat other players, spectators, and organizers with respect.

Natural beach conditions

Beach volleyball happens on a natural beach. Expect shells, uneven sand, changing conditions, and the normal hazards that come with playing outdoors.

Formats may vary

Tournament structure may change a little depending on registration, weather, beach conditions, or what makes the most sense for the event. If anything changes, we’ll keep everyone updated.

Partner requests

Free agents are welcome to share preferred partners or general teammate preferences. We’ll do our best to accommodate requests, but final pairings are made based on what works best for the tournament.

Event-specific rules

Net height, format, scoring, pool play, playoffs, and other details will be listed on each tournament page.

About MAD

Make A Day.

What started as beach volleyball with friends keeps turning into bigger and better days in the sand.

Why we do this

More than showing up for one match.

MAD is about making a day out of beach volleyball — good games, different people, new formats, and enough playing time that the tournament actually feels worth showing up for.

Some people come to compete. Some come to see friends. Most are here for some combination of both.

The tournaments are built around the community that keeps showing up, and they’ll keep evolving as that community grows.
